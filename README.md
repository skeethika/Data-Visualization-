📊 Problem Statement 01 – ABC Retail Mart

Topic

Business Intelligence Analysis

Objective

To understand how Business Intelligence and Data Visualization can help ABC Retail Mart identify the reasons behind declining sales and customer satisfaction.

Business Problem

ABC Retail Mart has experienced a decline in:

- Sales revenue
- Customer satisfaction
- Branch performance

The organization already collects information from sales, inventory, customer feedback, billing, and other departments. However, the information is stored separately, making it difficult for management to obtain a complete view of business performance.

BI Approach

The proposed BI solution includes:

- Centralizing data from different departments
- Integrating sales, inventory, billing, and customer feedback data
- Creating business KPIs
- Performing branch-wise and product-wise analysis
- Using drill-down analysis to identify possible causes
- Automating recurring reports
- Monitoring business performance continuously

Key KPIs

Some of the proposed KPIs include:

- Monthly Sales Revenue
- Sales Growth Rate
- Stock-out Rate
- Inventory Turnover Ratio
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Average Transaction Value
- Branch-wise Profitability
- Contribution Margin

Outcome

This problem statement demonstrates the role of Business Intelligence and Data Visualization in business decision-making.

🧹 Problem Statement 03 – Superstore Sales Dataset

Topic

Data Preparation and Transformation

Dataset

Sample Superstore Dataset

Objective

To prepare raw transactional data for Business Intelligence analysis and visualization.

Before creating visualizations, the dataset needs to be checked, cleaned, transformed, and organized into an analysis-ready format.

Data Preparation Activities

The following data preparation activities were covered:

1. Duplicate Identification

Duplicate transaction and line-item records were checked to prevent incorrect sales and profit calculations.

2. Missing Value Handling

The dataset was checked for missing information and appropriate handling methods were considered depending on the importance of each field.

3. Date Standardization

Order Date and Ship Date fields were standardized to support accurate time-based analysis.

4. Categorical Data Cleaning

Fields such as:

- Region
- Segment
- Category
- Sub-Category

were reviewed for consistency.

5. Calculated Columns

Additional metrics were created, including:

Profit %

Profit % = (Profit / Sales) × 100

Order Processing Time

Order Processing Time = Ship Date - Order Date

A Sales Category was also created to classify orders based on their sales value.

6. Data Aggregation

The data can be grouped and analyzed by:

- Region
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Month
- Quarter
- Customer Segment

Outcome

The raw Superstore dataset was transformed into a structured format suitable for Business Intelligence tools and data visualization.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard is designed to identify patterns such as:

Category Performance

Different product categories contribute differently to sales and profitability. Sales volume and profitability should therefore be analyzed separately.

Discount and Profitability

Higher discounts can be associated with lower profitability in certain transactions. This makes discount analysis important when evaluating sales performance.

Regional Performance

Sales are distributed differently across geographic regions and states. The dashboard allows management to identify areas with relatively high and low sales.

Seasonal Trends

Monthly sales analysis can be used to identify changes in demand over time and possible seasonal patterns.

Customer Segments

The dashboard compares the contribution of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customer segments.

Sales vs. Profit

High sales do not necessarily result in high profit. The scatter plot helps identify transactions or product groups where sales and profitability differ.

🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project proposes several possible business actions:

1. Control excessive discounting
   Review discount levels for products and sub-categories where discounts may significantly reduce profitability.

2. Review underperforming regions
   Examine lower-sales regions and identify opportunities for improved marketing and distribution.

3. Review low-margin products
   Analyze pricing, costs, and supplier terms for sub-categories with lower profitability.

4. Prepare for seasonal demand
   Use historical sales trends to support inventory and marketing planning during high-demand periods.

5. Develop customer-segment strategies
   Analyze lower-contributing customer segments and consider targeted marketing initiatives.

6. Monitor KPIs regularly
   Use the dashboard for periodic performance reviews and early identification of changes in business performance.
